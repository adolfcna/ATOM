# ATTvDEF – Cybersecurity Cheat Sheet

**ATTvDEF** is a comprehensive cybersecurity cheat sheet and study guide for security professionals. It focuses on **malware behavior, attack and defense techniques, reverse engineering, and practical scenarios**.

The project helps you understand how attacks are executed, how malware operates, and how defensive measures detect and mitigate threats. It is designed for hands-on learning and reference across both Red and Blue Team operations.

<Steps>
  1. **Explore Attack Techniques:** Understand how malware operates and maps to the MITRE ATT&CK framework.
  2. **Analyze Defenses:** Learn how Blue Teams detect, investigate, and mitigate threats.
  3. **Hands-On Execution:** Apply tools and scenarios in authorized lab environments.
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Commands & Tools" icon="terminal" defaultOpen>
    This section covers essential commands and tools used in offensive and defensive security operations.

    **Red Team tools** include Metasploit, Cobalt Strike, Empire, PowerShell, and Sysinternals for authorized penetration testing, exploitation research, and attack simulation.

    **Blue Team tools** include Splunk, ELK, Wireshark, SIEM platforms, and EDR solutions such as CrowdStrike and SentinelOne for monitoring, detection, and incident response.

    These references help security professionals execute authorized tests, investigate activity, and understand the telemetry generated by different tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Malware Behavior & TTPs" icon="shield-check" defaultOpen>
    ATT&DEF maps malware behavior and attack techniques to the **MITRE ATT&CK framework**, connecting offensive techniques with defensive detection opportunities.

    Explore how malware can establish persistence, escalate privileges, move laterally, and exfiltrate data. Reverse engineering examples help explain program internals, execution flow, suspicious behavior, and methods for identifying malicious indicators.

    Each topic should connect the relevant technique to observable artifacts, detection logic, and mitigation strategies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Scenarios & Attack Patterns" icon="search" defaultOpen>
    Study practical security scenarios ranging from initial access to post-compromise activity.

    Topics include privilege escalation, lateral movement, persistence, and defense evasion. Each scenario explains how the activity may unfold in an authorized lab or controlled assessment and how Blue Teams can detect, investigate, and mitigate it.

    The goal is to connect attack simulation with realistic defensive validation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Incident Response & Defensive Strategies" icon="shield" defaultOpen>
    This section focuses on investigating alerts, analyzing logs, and responding to malware or suspected Red Team activity.

    Key artifacts, telemetry sources, indicators of compromise (IOCs), and response procedures help analysts investigate incidents and determine appropriate containment and remediation actions.

    The emphasis is on turning knowledge of attack techniques into practical defensive capabilities.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Target Audience & Learning Value" icon="graduation-cap" defaultOpen>
    ATT&DEF is designed for Red Team operators, Blue Team analysts, SOC teams, penetration testers, malware analysts, reverse engineers, and cybersecurity students.

    It bridges offensive and defensive operations through hands-on exercises, malware analysis, scenario-based learning, and reverse engineering.

    The aim is to strengthen understanding of attack techniques, malware functionality, detection engineering, and effective defensive measures.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
